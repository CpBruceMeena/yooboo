# 🃏 Uno-No-Mercy — Official Rules

## Overview
Uno-No-Mercy is a high-stakes Uno variant with stacking penalties, special cards, and elimination. Last player standing with cards in hand wins — or the first to empty their hand.

## 3. Game Play

### Starting the Game
- Each player is dealt 7 cards
- The top card of the draw pile becomes the first discard
- If the first discard is a Wild/Special card, it's reshuffled until a number card appears
- The active color is set to the first discard's color

### Taking a Turn
1. **Play a card** — Match the color **OR** type/value of the top discard card
2. **Draw a card** — If you cannot (or choose not to) play, draw 1 card
3. **After drawing** — You may play a matching card **OR** skip your turn
4. **Draw only once** — You may only draw once per turn

### Matching Rules
- A card can be played if it matches the **color** OR **type** (number/action) of the top discard
- **Number cards** match by value (e.g., a red 5 can be played on a blue 5, changing the active color to blue)
- **Action cards** match by type (e.g., a red +2 can be played on a yellow +2, a green +4 on a blue +4)
- **Special cards** (Smiley, +4 Reverse, +6, +10) are color-independent and respect only the current color flow

### Active Color
- When any color-specific card is played (number, Reverse, +2, +4, Skip Everyone), the active color updates to that card's color
- When a Wild or Special card is played, the player **chooses** the new active color
- Playing a number card of the same value but **different color** changes the active color to the card's color

### Draw & Skip Rule
- You may draw **only once** per turn
- After drawing, if you have a matching card you can play it **OR** choose to skip your turn
- If you skip, the next player takes their turn

## 4. Card Effects

### Number Cards (0-9)
- No special effect
- Updates active color to the card's color

### Reverse
- Reverses direction of play
- With 2 players: acts as Skip (player who played goes again)
- Updates active color to the card's color

### +2 (Skip)
- Next player draws 2 cards and misses their turn
- **Stackable**: Another +2 can be played on top (+2→+4→+6...)
- Updates active color to the card's color

### +4 (Color-Specific)
- Next player draws 4 cards and misses their turn
- **Stackable**: Another +4 can be played on top (color may differ)
- Updates active color to the card's color

### Wild +4 Reverse
- **Direction reverses first**
- Next player draws 4 cards and misses their turn
- Player chooses the next color
- **Stackable**: Another +4 Reverse can be played on top

### Wild +6
- Next player draws 6 cards and misses their turn
- Player chooses the next color
- **Stackable**: Another +6 can be played on top

### Wild +10 (MAX DRAW)
- Next player draws 10 cards and misses their turn
- Player chooses the next color
- **Stackable**: Another +10 can be played on top

### Skip Everyone
- Every other player is skipped — the player who played goes again
- Cannot be played during a stack

### Discard All (Color-Specific)
- The discard card is played onto the discard pile (stays on top)
- The card's color becomes the active color
- Player may **optionally select which cards of that color** to discard (partial discard allowed)
- Player can also simply play the card alone with no discarding
- Since the discard card is on top, the next player can play either the **active color** OR a **Discard All** card

### Smiley (😊)
- Player chooses a color
- Next player draws cards from the draw pile **one-by-one** (animated reveal to all players)
- Drawing continues **until they draw the chosen color card**
- After each card drawn: if total hand is **≥25 cards**, that player is eliminated immediately
- If the draw pile empties without matching, drawing stops
- **Cannot be stacked**

## 5. Stacking Rules

### Color-Specific Stacking
- +2 can be stacked only on +2 (color may differ)
- Reverse can be stacked only on Reverse
- Skip can be stacked only on Skip

### Wild/Special Stacking
- +4 can be stacked only on +4 (color may differ)
- Wild +6 can be stacked only on Wild +6
- Wild +10 can be stacked only on Wild +10
- +4 Reverse can be stacked only on +4 Reverse
- Smiley cannot be stacked

### Illegal Stacking
- A Reverse cannot be stacked on a +4 Reverse (or vice versa)
- Different special card types cannot be stacked on each other

### Stack Resolution
- When a player cannot (or chooses not to) stack, they must draw **all stacked cards**
- The player who drew then misses their turn
- Example: +4→+4→+6 = 14 cards drawn by the unlucky player

## 6. Elimination & Winning

### Elimination
- When a player reaches **25+ cards** in hand, they are eliminated
- **Smiley draw:** Elimination is checked after **each drawn card** — if the limit is reached mid-draw, elimination happens immediately and drawing stops
- Eliminated players are removed from the game
- Game continues with remaining players

### Winning
- **First to empty their hand wins the game**
- OR **last player standing** after all others are eliminated

## 7. UNO Rule

- When a player plays their **2nd-to-last card** (leaving 1 card in hand), **UNO is automatically called**
- A notification is shown to **all players** when someone calls UNO
- If another player catches them before the next player's turn, they draw **2 penalty cards**

## 8. Color Flow Rules

- **Action cards** (Reverse, Skip, +2) keep the current color flow (unless a different-color same-type match changes it)
- **Wild cards** let the player choose the next color
- **Special cards** (Smiley, +4 Reverse, +6, +10) let the player choose the next color
- Playing a different-color number card of the same value changes the color flow
- **Discard All**: the discard card stays on top, so the next player matches by **chosen color** or **Discard All type**
